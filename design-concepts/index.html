CarMatch: 5 results-page concepts

Same 9 cars, same data (EPA specs, AI price estimates, Wikimedia photos). Click any car to see how the details view could work.

  1. 1 · Color ArcBased on your Audi reference: tall horizontal cards, colored arcs, thin/bold type, floating Save and share.
  2. 2 · Soft DashboardBased on your dashboard reference: soft light panels, best-match hero, stat tiles, an AI assistant panel with quick actions.
  3. 3 · Night ShowroomDark, cinematic: the selected car fills the screen with a match ring, a filmstrip of the 9, and a slide-in details panel.
  4. 4 · Swipe DeckMobile-first: one car at a time, swipe to save or skip, a bottom sheet for details, then compare your shortlist.
  5. 5 · Editorial ShortlistMagazine-style ranked list with big numbers, short verdicts, expandable reviews and a compare drawer.
